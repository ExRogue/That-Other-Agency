The problem it solves

Most ads start from nothing.

Brands tend to write ads one at a time. Each one starts from a blank page, and when one works nobody is quite sure why.

We build a creative bible for your brand first. It holds who you sell to, what they already believe, the proof you have and the words your customers use. Every script is built from it, so each ad tests a clear idea and each result teaches you something.

What you get

Everything needed to press record.

The strategy, written down

  • A creative bible. Your customer types, buying stages, angles, a bank of 100 hooks, script formulas, proof, customer language and answers to the usual objections.
  • A voice pack. How your founder or presenter really talks, taken from their own words, so nothing sounds like it was written by a committee.
  • A claims check. Every line is checked against the proof you hold. Nothing is made up to sound good.

The scripts, ready to film

  • A script pack. Long scripts plus 30 second cutdowns, each labelled with the angle it tests.
  • A recording guide. Framing, lighting, delivery and a shot list for each script, so someone who is not a presenter can film it alone.
  • Reference links. Real examples of the delivery style we are aiming for.

How it works

Four steps. Then it repeats.

01

Research

We read your reviews, study your competitors and past ads, and spend time with the people who know your customer best.

02

Build the bible

Everything we learn goes into one structured document that your whole team can use.

03

Write and check

Scripts are drafted on proven structures, rewritten to sound spoken, then checked against the bible before you see them.

04

Film, test, learn

You film and run the ads. What wins and what flops goes back into the bible and shapes the next pack.

Common questions

Before you ask.

Does it only work with a founder on camera?

No. The same system writes for creators, customers, voiceover and static ads. A founder is often the strongest option, so that is where we usually start.

Who owns the creative bible?

You do. It is your document, and your team can use it for emails, landing pages and briefs as well as ads.

Can our own team run it after setup?

Yes. If you would rather write in house, we can set the system up, train your team and hand it over.

Do you run the ads as well?

We can. Paid acquisition is part of our ongoing growth support, and the two work well together.
