Common questions

Before you ask.

Can you work alongside our team?

Yes. We can run everything, or take the parts your team doesn’t have time for, such as the ads, the emails or the store changes.

What do you need from us?

Access to Shopify, your ad accounts and Klaviyo, and a few minutes each week to agree priorities. We do the setup ourselves.

Which ad platforms do you run?

Meta, Google and TikTok for most brands, and LinkedIn for B2B. We start where your customers already are.

Is there a contract?

Monthly support runs month to month, with 30 days’ notice either side. The monthly quote is fixed after the consultation.

Do we need a dashboard?

It helps, because every change we make gets marked on it. We can build you a custom dashboard, or you can use That Other Dashboard, our Shopify app.
