That Other Quiz · Your data

Deleting your data, step by step.

Last updated 30 October 2026

The short version: delete a quiz in That Other Quiz and its counts and orders are deleted straight away. Uninstall That Other Quiz and everything else is deleted within 48 hours, apart from our own email list, which keeps your store’s name and email for up to 12 months. Shoppers’ emails were never with us: they are in your own Klaviyo. Or email us and we will do it for you.

Delete one quiz

  1. In your Shopify admin, open Apps, then That Other Quiz, then Quizzes.
  2. Click Delete next to the quiz, then Delete again to confirm.

The quiz is removed from your store, and That Other Quiz deletes its counts and the records of orders that came from it, straight away. Any block or popup that showed it shows your main quiz instead.

Stop sending shoppers to Klaviyo

  1. Open the quiz in That Other Quiz’s editor and choose Klaviyo.
  2. Clear the Public site ID and List ID, or set the Email step so the quiz doesn’t ask for an email, then save.

Emails and answers that shoppers already gave are in your Klaviyo account, not with us. To delete them, delete those profiles in Klaviyo.

What stays in your store

  • The discount for quiz takers. On That Other Quiz’s home page, click Stop the code and the quiz stops showing it. The code itself is a Shopify discount: delete it under Discounts in your Shopify admin.
  • Pictures you uploaded. They are in your own Shopify Files and stay there after you uninstall. Delete them under Content, then Files.

Stop our emails

Click Unsubscribe at the bottom of any email from us about That Other Quiz. We keep your store’s name and email address in our email list for up to 12 months after you uninstall; email us to have them deleted sooner.

Delete everything

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Settings, then Apps.
  2. Find That Other Quiz and uninstall it.

Shopify removes the quiz from your theme and deletes your quizzes, which are kept as the app’s own data in your store. Your store’s login to That Other Quiz is deleted at once. 48 hours later Shopify tells us to delete the rest, and we delete the records of orders that came from your quizzes, their counts and your AI builds. The gap lets you reinstall without losing your history if you change your mind. Deleted data can stay in our database provider’s backups for up to 7 days.

Ask us to do it

Email hello@thatotheragency.co.uk with your store’s address (yourstore.myshopify.com) and what you would like deleted: one quiz’s counts and orders, or everything. We will check the request comes from the store, delete the data within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm by email.

If you took a store’s quiz

That Other Quiz keeps no personal details about the shoppers who take a store’s quiz. If you gave your email, it went to the store’s own Klaviyo: ask the store to delete it. If you bought something after the quiz, we keep a record of that order for the store’s report, without your name or contact details, and delete it when you ask the store and Shopify passes your request on.

While a store tests two versions of its quiz, and only if you agreed to analytics, your browser remembers which version you saw. Clearing the shop’s site data in your browser deletes it.
