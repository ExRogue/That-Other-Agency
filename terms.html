Terms of business

Our terms, in plain English.

Last updated 11 October 2026

The short version: your proposal says what we'll do and what it costs. You pay a setup fee once, then a monthly fee by Direct Debit. Either of us can stop with 30 days' notice by email. Your data stays yours, and we only read what your service needs.

Who we are

That Other Agency Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales, company number 16799053 ("we" and "us"). "You" means the business that hires us. These terms apply to every service we provide, together with the proposal we send you.

How our agreement works

Your proposal sets out what we'll do, the fees, and anything agreed just for you. These terms cover everything else. If the two ever disagree, the proposal wins.

Our agreement starts when you accept the proposal in writing (an email is fine), set up your Direct Debit, or pay the setup fee, whichever comes first.

Fees and payment

  • The setup fee is paid once, at the start. It pays for building and connecting your service, so it isn't refundable once we've started that work.
  • The monthly fee is paid in advance each month by Direct Debit through GoCardless. GoCardless emails you before every payment.
  • VAT. Our fees don't include VAT. If VAT applies, we'll add it at the current rate and show it on your invoice.
  • Missed payments. If a payment fails, we'll tell you and try again. If a payment is still unpaid 14 days after it was due, we may pause the service until it's paid.
  • Price changes. We may change the monthly fee by giving you at least 30 days' notice by email. If you don't want to continue at the new price, you can end the service before it applies.

What the monthly fee covers

For a dashboard, your monthly fee covers:

  • hosting your dashboard and keeping it online
  • keeping the connections to your tools working, including when a platform changes how its data works
  • fixing anything in your dashboard that stops working
  • answering reasonable questions about using it

It doesn't cover new panels, new tabs, new data sources or other new features. We're happy to build them. We'll quote first, and only start once you agree. For our other services, your proposal sets out what's included.

Your part

  • Give us the access the service needs, and keep it in place while we work for you.
  • Tell us about changes that affect the service, such as a new ad account or a new store.
  • Make sure you're allowed to give us access to the accounts and data involved.

If access arrives late or is removed, timings move back and the service may stop working until it's restored. That isn't a breach on our side.

Your data

Your data stays yours. We only read what the service needs. We never sell it, never share it with other clients, and never publish your figures.

When we handle personal data for you, such as your customers' orders, we act as your data processor under UK data protection law. That means we will:

  • only use it to provide the service, and only on your instructions
  • make sure anyone who works with it keeps it confidential
  • keep it secure, behind passwords, with reputable providers
  • store it only with the providers we use to run the service (Vercel for hosting, and Upstash or Redis for a dashboard's saved figures), use the safeguards UK law requires where they store it outside the UK, and tell you before we add another provider
  • help you answer requests from people about their data, and tell you without delay if anything goes wrong with it
  • delete it when the service ends, unless the law requires us to keep it
  • give you the information you need to show all of this is being done

Our privacy notice explains how we handle your own team's details.

Confidentiality

We each keep the other's confidential information private, while we work together and after we stop. We'll only name you as a client, or show your work, with your permission.

Who owns what

Your data, your brand and anything you give us remain yours.

The code behind our dashboards and tools is ours. We reuse and improve it for all our clients, which is how fixes reach everyone. Nothing of yours is ever shared through it.

You can use your dashboard for as long as your monthly service runs. When it ends, we'll send you an export of anything you've saved in it, such as your board, notes and targets, if you ask within 30 days.

Ending the service

  • 30 days' notice. Either of us can end the monthly service by giving the other 30 days' notice by email. The monthly fee is due for the notice period.
  • Cancelling the Direct Debit doesn't end our agreement by itself, so please email us too.
  • Ending straight away. We can end the service immediately if a payment is more than 30 days late. Either of us can end it immediately if the other seriously breaks these terms and doesn't put it right within 14 days of being asked.
  • When it ends, we switch off your dashboard, remove our access to your tools (please remove any access you gave us too), and delete your data within 30 days, apart from what we must keep for our accounts. The setup fee isn't refunded.

Liability

  • We take care over every figure and check it against its source. The service reads from your tools, though, so it can only be as accurate as they are, and the decisions you make with it are yours.
  • We aren't responsible for problems caused by the platforms we connect to, such as an outage or a change they make without warning. We'll fix our side as quickly as we reasonably can.
  • We aren't liable for indirect losses, or for lost profits, sales or opportunities.
  • Our total liability to you under our agreement is limited to the fees you paid us in the 12 months before the claim.
  • Nothing in these terms limits any liability the law doesn't allow us to limit, such as for fraud, or for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. We'll email you at least 30 days before a change affects you, and the date at the top of this page shows when they last changed.

Law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales deal with any dispute. If something goes wrong, please talk to us first. Most things can be fixed with an email.
