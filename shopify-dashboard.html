Common questions

Before you ask.

Is our data safe?

Yes. The dashboard is private and password protected. It only reads your data, it is connected to your accounts alone, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

What do you need from us?

A Shopify store, read only access to the tools you want connected, and half an hour to agree which numbers matter most.

How much does it cost?

Every dashboard is quoted after the demo call, because it depends on how many tools you want connected. You get a fixed quote before anything starts, and the dashboard is yours to keep.

How long does it take?

Most dashboards are live within a few weeks of the call. The biggest factor is how quickly access to each tool can be granted.

We are not on Shopify. Can you still help?

The dashboard is built around Shopify today. Tell us what you sell on and we will say honestly whether we can do it.

Can we see it before we talk?

Yes. The live demo is the real dashboard with a made up brand. Click around as much as you like.
