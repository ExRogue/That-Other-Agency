Welcome aboard

Three steps, then we start building.

Thanks for choosing us. This page gets everything ready so we can get going. It takes about ten minutes, and you only do it once.

Step 1

Set up your Direct Debit.

Two minutes on a secure form

We take payments by Direct Debit through GoCardless. You fill in your bank details on their secure form, never on our site, and we never see your full account number.

The amounts are the ones in your proposal: the setup fee once, then your monthly fee.

Set up Direct Debit ↗︎

Your Direct Debit link is in the email we sent with your proposal. Can't find it? Email hello@thatotheragency.co.uk and we'll send it again.

What to expect

  • GoCardless emails you before every payment, so nothing is ever a surprise.
  • In the UK, every payment is covered by the Direct Debit Guarantee.
  • Outside the UK? GoCardless covers Direct Debit in several other countries. If yours isn't one of them, email us and we'll arrange another way to pay.
  • Setting it up means you agree to our terms.

Step 2

Give us access to the tools in your proposal.

We do all the setup. All we need is a way in. Everything the dashboard connects to is read only, and private to your team.

Shopify

Add us as staff

In Shopify, go to Settings, then Users, and add hello@thatotheragency.co.uk as a staff member with permission to manage and develop apps. The dashboard uses a private app that only reads your data.

Meta

Add us to your business

In Meta Business Settings, add hello@thatotheragency.co.uk as a person with full control, so we can set up the read only connection inside your business. You can lower it to partial access once your dashboard is live.

Google Ads

Add one read only user

We'll email you a dashboard address. In Google Ads, go to Admin, then Access and security, and add it as a user with Read only access.

Klaviyo

Add us as a user

Add hello@thatotheragency.co.uk as a user with the Manager role, so we can create a read only key for the dashboard.

Using Recharge, TikTok or something else? We'll email you the steps for those. If anything looks different on your screen, send us a screenshot and we'll guide you through it.

Step 3

What happens next.

01

We build it

As soon as access comes through, we connect your tools and build your dashboard around the numbers we agreed.

02

We check every figure

Each number is checked against its source before you rely on it. You get your private link and password, usually within a week of the last access arriving.

03

We keep it running

Your monthly fee covers hosting, keeping every connection working when the platforms change, and fixing anything that breaks. New panels are quoted first, and only built once you say yes.
