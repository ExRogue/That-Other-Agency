Case study · Elite Southwest

A full rebuild that sells a specialist trade UK wide.

Elite Southwest are electro mechanical engineers for the water and food industries, based in Devon. Their old Wix site did not show what they could do or reach the clients they wanted. We rebuilt it from the ground up.

In their words

The project

Where they started

A capable engineering firm with a website that undersold it.

  • The site sat on a monthly Wix plan
  • Specialist services were hard to find
  • They wanted work from across the UK, not only the South West

What we did

We planned the site around how their customers search, then built every page from scratch.

  • A page for each specialist service
  • Pages for the sectors and counties they cover
  • Their own site photos, tidied and placed with care

What we built

A site built to win work.

Nine service pages

From confined space entry to hot tapping, each service has its own page written in plain English.

Sector and area pages

Pages for the water and food industries, and for Devon, Cornwall and Somerset, so the right searches find them.

Projects that prove it

Real jobs written up with real photos, so new clients can see the standard of work.

Quotes and calls

A quote form and tap to call on every page, tested end to end before launch.

No platform fee

The domain stayed where it was. The monthly website plan could be cancelled.

Set up for Google

Sitemap submitted and the site verified with Google on launch day.

Where it is now

Live since October 2026.

The new site went live with no downtime and no change to their email. Elite Southwest now have a site that shows the full range of what they do, and one they do not pay a platform to keep online.
