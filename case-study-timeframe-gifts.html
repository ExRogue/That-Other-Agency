Case study · TimeFrame Gifts

An online store for keepsakes that come to life.

TimeFrame Gifts prints your favourite photo on glass, acrylic or canvas, then plays your video over it when you scan a code. We built their Shopify store, from the first page to the checkout.

Shopify storeProduct personaliserShop by occasionGift cards
Live at timeframegifts.com

Where they started

A clever product that is hard to explain in a photo.

  • Every order needs the customer’s own photo and video
  • Shoppers had to understand how it works in seconds
  • Most visitors arrive on a phone

What we did

We built the store around one question: can a first time visitor understand it and order without help?

  • A clear three step explanation on every product
  • Photo upload and a live preview before buying
  • Collections for the moments people buy for

What we built

Built to explain. Built to sell.

Personalise before you buy

Customers upload a photo, add a name and see their own keepsake before it goes in the basket.

Shop by occasion

Collections for pets, new babies, weddings, school leavers and more, each led by the right photos.

How it works, made simple

A short demo and three plain steps, so the moving photo makes sense straight away.

Gift cards and offers

Gift cards with offers for buying several, for people who want to give the choice.

Made for phones

Every page was checked and tuned at phone size, from the photo galleries to the basket.

Trust up front

Delivery, how photos are checked and what arrives by email, shown before anyone has to ask.

Where it is now

Live and taking shape.

The store is live with the full range of keepsakes, and we keep improving it as the business grows.
