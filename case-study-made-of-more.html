Case study · Made Of More

Growth for a skincare brand, run month by month.

Made Of More makes skincare for midlife skin, sold on Shopify and fronted by its founder. We run their growth month by month: the ads and the scripts behind them, offers and store changes, a skin quiz built into their theme, and a dashboard that shows what each change did.

Live at madeofmore.co.uk

The project

Where they started

A skincare brand with a founder people trust, a loyal audience and a small team.

  • Selling on Shopify, with ads on Meta and Google
  • A skin quiz running on a separate paid app
  • Sales, ad spend and email each in their own tool

What we did

We work as their growth team, with a short list of priorities agreed every week.

  • Offers, ads and founder scripts planned together
  • Store changes and the quiz built into their theme
  • Every change marked on one dashboard

What we built

The pieces that work together.

Founder scripts

A creative bible for the brand, then a full pack of long scripts and cutdowns in the founder’s voice, with a recording guide so she could film them herself.

See the script system

Ads and offers

Meta and Google campaigns, with the offers planned alongside them: bundle savings, a free gift and discount codes set up to work together at checkout, and a free delivery threshold test.

A skin quiz in the theme

A quiz built into their Shopify theme in place of a separate paid app. Answers go to Klaviyo for the follow up emails, results go straight to the cart, and the results pages come in more than one layout so they can be tested.

See quizzes

A custom dashboard

Shopify, Meta, Google Ads and Klaviyo in one private view, with free press and influencer orders kept apart from real sales, refunds by product, and the quiz’s starts and completions alongside.

See custom dashboards

Landing pages and the cart

A Rescue Balm landing page rebuilt from sections the team can edit themselves, and fixes to the cart drawer.

See Shopify development

Weekly priorities

A short list in Slack every week, so the team always knows what is being done and why.

See growth support

Where it is now

Ongoing, week by week.

We still run Made Of More’s growth every month. The quiz has been live in their theme since October 2026, and the dashboard is where the team checks what each change did.
