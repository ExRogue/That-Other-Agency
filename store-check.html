What we look at

Six places sales slip away.

All checked from the outside, the way your customers see your store. All we need is the address.

01

Tracking

Which tags are on your store, from the Meta pixel and GA4 to Google Ads and TikTok, what each one sends from product view to checkout, and whether they wait for your cookie banner as they should.

02

Speed on a phone

Lighthouse, the engine behind Google PageSpeed, on a phone for your homepage, a collection and a product page, plus the files and app scripts slowing them down.

03

Product pages on a phone

Is the offer clear before anyone scrolls? We look for reviews, delivery and returns details, a sticky add to cart button and express checkout such as Shop Pay, Apple Pay, PayPal and Klarna.

04

Offer and basket

Your free delivery threshold against your typical prices, offers stacked on top of each other, sold out products on show and what your basket suggests next.

05

Email capture

Is there a signup form or popup, when does it show, and what does it offer someone on their first visit?

06

Your live ads

From Meta’s Ad Library and Google’s Ads Transparency Center: how many ads are live, in which formats, how long they’ve run, and whether each one lands on the page it shows.

What you get

A short report. Three fixes first.

A PDF of about six pages. Page one gives each area a traffic light and lists the three fixes we’d make first, ranked by likely effect and effort. Then a page per area: what we found, with screenshots, and what to do about it.

Every finding is checked by hand on your live store before it goes out, and we never promise results.

The account check

View only. Never passwords.

For brands spending £5k or more a month on ads, we can look inside your Meta and Google Ads accounts too: how they’re set up, where the spend goes, how often people see the same ad, tired creative, wasted search terms, Performance Max taking credit for searches of your own name, and conversions counted twice.

We also set your ad spend against your Shopify sales over the last 90 days.

How view only access works

You give us access from your own accounts, so we never need a password, and you can remove us at any time.

  • Meta: add our business portfolio as a partner on your ad account, with “View performance” only.
  • Google Ads: invite hello@thatotheragency.co.uk with “Read only” access.
  • Your sales: a screenshot of your Shopify Analytics is enough.
  • Afterwards: we come off within 14 days of sending your report.

Tick the box on the form and we’ll email you the exact steps.

Common questions

No catch. No chasing.

Is it really free?

Yes. No card and no catch. We do a few each week because it’s the best way to show how we work, and the report is yours to keep. If we’re full, we’ll tell you the day yours will arrive.

Do you need my passwords?

No. The store check only needs your store address. The account check uses view only access that you give us and can remove at any time.

Will you chase me?

Once. We send one follow up after your report, in case you have questions. Then nothing.

Who sees my numbers?

Only us. Your report and anything we see in your accounts stay between you and us, and are never published. We delete our notes after six months unless you become a client.

What if we already work with an agency?

That’s fine. Treat it as a second opinion. The fixes are written so your own team or your agency can act on them.
