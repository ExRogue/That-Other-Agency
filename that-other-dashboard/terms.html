That Other Dashboard · Terms

That Other Dashboard’s terms, in plain English.

Last updated 14 October 2026

The short version: That Other Dashboard reads your store and the tools you connect, and shows them on one screen. The free plan is free. The paid plan starts with a 14 day free trial, then Shopify bills it each month until you cancel. Your data stays yours, and you can stop at any time by uninstalling.

These terms

These terms are an agreement between your business and That Other Agency Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 16799053 (“we”), for using That Other Dashboard, our Shopify app. By installing That Other Dashboard you agree to them on behalf of your business, and you confirm you can make that decision for it.

That Other Dashboard is for businesses, not consumers. Shopify’s own terms, and the terms of each tool you connect, apply alongside these.

What That Other Dashboard does

That Other Dashboard reads orders from your Shopify store and reports from the tools you connect, such as Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, Klaviyo and Recharge, and shows them on one dashboard. It only reads. It never changes anything in your store or your accounts.

The figures come from those platforms. They can count sales differently, credit the same sale to more than one channel, change past figures as their attribution settles, or arrive late. That Other Dashboard shows what they report, and works out figures such as MER and cost per order from them. It is information to help you decide, not financial or business advice, and the decisions you make with it are yours.

Plans and billing

  • The free plan is free. If we ever take a feature away from it, we will tell you in That Other Dashboard at least 30 days before.
  • The paid plan has a monthly fee, shown on That Other Dashboard’s Shopify App Store listing and in the app before you choose it, plus any tax Shopify applies.
  • Free trial. Your first 14 days on the paid plan are free, once per store. If you don’t cancel before the trial ends, the paid plan carries on and the fee starts.
  • Billing. Shopify charges the fee on your Shopify bill every 30 days, and Shopify’s billing terms apply to the charge. We never see your card details.
  • Cancelling. Cancel at any time by switching to the free plan or uninstalling That Other Dashboard. Shopify applies its own rules to any part of a month. Otherwise fees are not refundable, unless the law says they must be.
  • Price changes. We will tell you in That Other Dashboard at least 30 days before a price rise, and you can cancel before it applies.

Your part

  • Decide who in your business can open That Other Dashboard in your Shopify admin, and keep your logins to the tools you connect safe.
  • Only connect accounts you are allowed to connect, and keep to the terms of each platform.
  • Don’t misuse That Other Dashboard: no attempts to break it, overload it or get round its security, no copying or reselling it, and nothing unlawful.

Your data

Your data stays yours. You allow us to use it only to run That Other Dashboard for you, as our privacy policy explains. We never sell it, never share it with other merchants and never publish your figures.

That Other Dashboard keeps no details of your customers. Disconnecting a tool deletes what That Other Dashboard read from it straight away, and uninstalling deletes everything else, as deleting your data describes.

Where That Other Dashboard handles personal data about your customers (order references and order totals, never names, emails, phone numbers or addresses), we do it as your processor under UK and EU data protection law: only on your instructions, which are these terms, and only to run That Other Dashboard for you. We keep it confidential, keep it secure, use only the providers our privacy policy lists, help you answer your customers’ requests, tell you without undue delay if anything goes wrong with it, and delete it when you uninstall.

Our part

We run That Other Dashboard with reasonable care and skill, and fix problems as quickly as we reasonably can. We can’t promise it will always be available or free of errors, because it depends on Shopify, our hosting providers and the platforms you connect, which change their systems from time to time.

We keep improving That Other Dashboard, so features may change. If we ever close That Other Dashboard, we will give you at least 30 days’ notice in the app and stop charging you.

Ending

You can stop using That Other Dashboard at any time by uninstalling it. We may suspend or end your use of That Other Dashboard if you seriously break these terms, misuse it, or Shopify stops your store or our app, and we will tell you why where we can.
