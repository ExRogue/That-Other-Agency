The problem it solves

An app for every idea. A slower store.

Most stores add an app for each new idea: a quiz, a bundle, a gift, a pop up. Each one brings a monthly fee and a little more weight on every page.

We build those features into your theme instead, check them on phones first and measure what each one did.

Common questions

Before you ask.

Do you work on our live theme?

Carefully. We back up your theme before every change, check the work on a preview first and publish once you’re happy.

What access do you need?

Staff access to your Shopify admin with the permissions the work needs. We do the setup ourselves.

Can you work with our developer or agency?

Yes. We can take on a list of changes alongside them, or hand over everything we build.

Will it slow our store down?

Usually the opposite. We build features into your theme instead of adding apps, so pages tend to get lighter.

Do you build Shopify apps?

We do: That Other Dashboard is our own. For your store, though, we usually build into the theme, which is quicker and costs nothing a month.
