Who it is for

For brands with something to sell.

Maybe you are launching your first products and need a proper store, not a template with your logo dropped in. Or your store has grown in bits: a theme bought years ago, a stack of apps doing small jobs, and pages that crawl on a phone.

Either way, we plan the store around how your customers shop, then design and build it on Shopify. You end up with a store you can run yourself, ready for ads, email and search.

What a good store needs

The parts that turn visits into orders.

A Shopify store lives or dies on a few pages: the product page, the collection page and the basket. That is where we spend our time.

Product pages that answer questions

Clear photos, sizes, delivery times and returns, close to the add to basket button. Every question a shopper has to email about is an order at risk.

Collections people can browse

Shop by range, by need or by occasion, with filters that work with a thumb. Shoppers should reach the right product in a couple of taps.

Built for phones first

Plenty of shoppers arrive on a phone, straight from an ad or an email. We design and check every page at phone size first, from the gallery to the basket.

Quick pages, fewer apps

Every app adds weight and often a monthly bill. We use what Shopify does well on its own first, and only add apps that earn their place.

The harder parts, done properly

Products customers personalise before they buy, photo uploads with a live preview, and gift cards. Built to be easy to use and easy for you to manage.

Set up for Google

Page titles, descriptions and product details Google can read, clean web addresses, and the sitemap submitted at launch.

Common questions

Before you ask.

Do you only build stores on Shopify?

For online stores, yes. Shopify handles payments, stock and orders well, and it is easy for you to run day to day. If you need a site that does not sell online, see our website design service.

Can you redesign the Shopify store we already have?

Yes. We build the new design on an unpublished theme inside your store, so the current one stays live while we work. Your products, customers and orders do not move. When you are happy, we publish it.

We are on Wix, Squarespace or WooCommerce. Can you move us to Shopify?

Yes. You keep your domain. We bring your products across and redirect old page addresses, so existing links still land somewhere useful. The plan sets out exactly what moves and what does not.

How long does a Shopify build take?

It depends on how many products you have and the features you need. You get a timeline in the written plan before anything starts. The biggest factor is usually how quickly photos and product details come through.

Will we own the store?

Yes. The Shopify account is in your name, you pay Shopify directly for your plan and any apps, and the design and content are yours. There is no contract tying you to us.

Can you help us grow once it is live?

Yes. Our ongoing growth support covers strategy, paid ads, creative testing and conversion. We can also build a performance dashboard that puts your Shopify sales, ads and email in one place.
