Our own venture · Monitus

An AI growth manager for insurtechs, built in house.

Monitus is a software product we started with a co-founder. It reads the insurance market and turns it into a weekly brief, with posts and pitches drafted in the company’s own voice, for insurtechs without a marketing team. We designed and built it, from the first prototype to a working app. It is paused for now.

The Monitus landing page in June 2026. The market signals shown are samples.

The product

The problem

Insurtechs selling to insurers and brokers often have a good product and no marketing team. The market moves every day, and staying credible means keeping up with it.

  • Too many sources to read each week
  • No time to turn news into posts and pitches
  • Generic content that specialist buyers see through

What we built

A product that does the reading and hands back what matters, with a first draft ready to use.

  • A company profile built from the company’s own website
  • Insurance news and commentary grouped into market conversations
  • A weekly market brief with drafts in the company’s voice

A sample market brief. Every signal and draft shown is an example.

How we built it

From an idea to a working app.

Product first

We worked out who it was for and what they needed each week before building, then shaped everything around that weekly brief.

Built with AI tools

A full web app built with AI development tools, which is how we build quickly for clients too.

Runs on its own

The brief is gathered and prepared automatically, so it is ready each week without anyone pressing a button.

Easy to start

Sign in, add a website address and get a company profile to work from.

Tested before launch

We tested the pitch and the product before spending on a launch, and changed direction when the answers said so.

A landing page that sells it

Clear positioning for a specialist buyer, with a sample brief that shows the value before anyone signs up.

Where it is now

Paused, and still useful.

Monitus is on hold while we focus on client work. Building it taught us how to turn a lot of information into one clear weekly view, which is what our dashboards do for online brands.
