Case study · Ventete

Meta ads for a helmet that folds flat.

Ventete makes a foldable bike helmet for people who cycle in the city. From October 2025 into early 2026 we ran their Meta ads on a monthly retainer: the plan, the campaigns, the changes each week and a clear note on what to do next.

The project

The brief

Run Ventete’s paid social month by month, with their own team supplying the creative.

  • Plan the account structure and audiences
  • Brief the creative with clear ideas to test
  • Report every week on what to do next

What we did

We set up and ran their Meta ads, and adjusted them every week.

  • A paid media brief and campaign structure
  • Hook ideas built around the commute and the look
  • A weekly commentary with actions, and a weekly call

What we delivered

A plan, the campaigns and a weekly read.

A paid media brief

Who to reach, what to say and how to judge it, agreed before the campaigns went live.

Campaigns and audiences

Meta campaigns and audiences set up and managed, with budgets paced through each month.

Hook ideas

Opening lines written around how people actually use the helmet: the commute, the bag it folds into and the outfit it doesn’t ruin.
