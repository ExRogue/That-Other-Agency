The problem it solves

Five tools. Five different answers.

Your shop says one thing, your ad accounts say another, and email claims the same sale as both. Most teams end up stitching it together in a spreadsheet on a Monday morning.

We put it in one place, built around the numbers your business actually runs on. Then we mark every change on the charts, so you can see what happened after it.
