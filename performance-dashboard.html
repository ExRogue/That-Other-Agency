The problem it solves

Five tools. Five different answers.

Your shop says one thing, your ad accounts say another, and email claims the same sale as both. Most teams end up stitching it together in a spreadsheet on a Monday morning.

We put it in one place, built around the numbers your business actually runs on. Then we mark every change on the charts, so you can see what happened after it.

What’s included

Built around your decisions.

The headline numbers

Sales, new customers, returning share, ad spend, cost per new customer and the return on everything you spend. Today, this week, this month.

Every change on the chart

Log a new page, an offer or a campaign and it appears as a marker on every chart. Before and after, without the guesswork.

Connected to your tools

Shopify, Meta, Google Ads, Klaviyo and Recharge. It reads your data and never changes anything.

Pace against your plan

See how the month is tracking against your targets while there is still time to do something about it.

Stock and run rate

Weekly run rate and months of cover for each product, so a best seller does not quietly run out.

Email and subscriptions

Campaign results beside sales, plus subscriptions started, cancelled and where people drop off.

Common questions

Before you ask.

Is our data safe?

Yes. The dashboard is private and password protected. It only reads your data, it is connected to your accounts alone, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

What do you need from us?

A Shopify store, read only access to the tools you want connected, and half an hour to agree which numbers matter most.

How is it priced?

Every dashboard is quoted after a consultation, because the work depends on how many tools you want connected. You get a fixed quote before we begin. If you want new panels and changes over time, there is an optional care plan.

We are not on Shopify. Can you still help?

The dashboard is built around Shopify today. If you sell somewhere else, tell us what you use and we will say honestly whether we can do it.
