About

A small team that runs growth and writes the code.

That Other Agency is a London team that works with Shopify brands and small businesses. We run ads, email and conversion, and we build what that needs ourselves: stores, quizzes, dashboards and our own Shopify apps.

Who we are

Set up in London in 2025.

Small on purpose

The people you talk to are the people who do the work. No account managers and no hand offs.

Builders who run growth

When a test needs a new page, a quiz or a tracking fix, we build it ourselves. Nothing waits for another agency.

Numbers you can see

Every change we make is marked on a dashboard, so you can see what it did, and what it didn’t.

How we work

A sprint, monthly support or one build.

Every project starts with a consultation, and a look at your store, ads and numbers before we suggest anything. Then you get a plan and a fixed quote.

A sprint

A set piece of work over a few weeks, such as a store fix list, a quiz or a checkout review.

Monthly support

Ongoing work on your ads, email and store, with priorities agreed every week.
